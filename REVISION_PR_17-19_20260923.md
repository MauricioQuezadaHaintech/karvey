## Executive Summary

`team-adapters` reached production (3.10.0 -> 3.11.1) with `approvals.prod` recorded and **no `approvals.qa`**,
a process failure found by the expert panel (H-22). This retroactive QA ran the 9 dimensions over the whole
range. After deduplication across dimensions there are **48 findings: 0 critical, 5 high, 16 medium, 27 low**
(17 bug, 23 spec-gap, 8 emergent).

The most urgent problem was live: the session hook showed, in ~60 `docs/spec` folders (most of them not
Karvey projects), a notice recommending `/karvey:karvey-init --settings`, and that command did not stop
after the settings step, so it could create a phantom change and a **real Epic in a client's tracker**
(BUG-01). That and three other defects with active impact (BUG-02 nudge scope/parsing/hang, BUG-03
statusline crash, BUG-04 cross-user debug file) are fixed in hotfix 3.11.2 with a regression suite
(`plugins/karvey/hooks/tests/test-hooks.sh`, 21/21 on the fix, 15/21 failing against the 3.11.1 hooks in a clean, isolated TMPDIR).

Four **high** findings remain open: the "missing status map" contract is only in the rule (F-05); no skill
moves leaf items to `done` (F-06), which makes impl's dependency/resume rule ambiguous (BUG-05, F-07); and the
new object-shaped `project.json:management` collides with the string already present in 16 HainTech repos,
so a `!= markdown` guard sends Markdown repos to a tracker (BUG-06, F-08). The second opinion (intra-model
fallback) returns FAIL against REQ-ADP-002 and REQ-ADP-022 in practice.

**Security gate: PASSES** (no critical or high security finding). **Verdict: NOT APPROVED** because of the
open highs. The remaining bugs (BUG-05..BUG-17) and all spec-gaps are routed to `wave1-hardening`; the
emergent findings go to the backlog (BL-38..BL-43, plus existing BL-07 and BL-10).
